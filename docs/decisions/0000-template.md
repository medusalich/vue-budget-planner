Short main clauses, one thought each. A sentence that only labels or rates another one says
nothing the record needs. An argument stands in the record it belongs to; anywhere else a link
to that number does it.

A new record also gets a line in the index, `README.md`.

## Context

The situation that forced a choice: what was true beforehand, which constraints applied,
which options were open. Facts, not the course of the discussion.

## Decision

The choice itself, in one or two sentences, in the present tense — "Amounts are stored as …",
not "we will store amounts as …".

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes harder, and which price is accepted knowingly.
Both directions belong here: a record listing only advantages is one that was not thought through.
